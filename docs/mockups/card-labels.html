Frozen graph labels

The same two frozen graphs, labelled four ways. Charts are the real gallery charts. The first row is what the cards say today.

Today

Numbers and signs a student has to decode.

A. Chips with counted squares

Each change is a chip. The square takes the colour of what changed (blue demand, orange supply, red policy), and the number of filled squares is the size of the change, the way an Isotype chart counts units. The words carry the direction.

B. Chips, words only

The same chips with the counting removed. Quieter, and the words alone say how big the change is.

C. One plain sentence

No chips: the changes read as a line, each led by its colour square. Takes the least room but is the least scannable when four or five graphs sit side by side.
