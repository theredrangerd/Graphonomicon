# Surface brief: Graphonomicon brand identity (homepage + shared chrome)

Mode: Operate (homepage hero is the brand's one loud moment). Audience: G11 IB Econ students; jobs: find a graph, share a link.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Graphonomicon is a classroom wall chart: flat ink, bold condensed caps, one idea per panel. Refuses the white edtech card page with a serif headline.
OWN-WORLD: Neurath-style Isotype wall chart. Warm poster-stock ground (#f2ead7), near-black ink (#1d1d1b), the existing demand blue, supply orange, gov purple as flat colour; Barlow Condensed 800 caps for wordmark and display, IBM Plex Sans stays for UI. A three-bar rising mark in blue/orange/purple is the logo and favicon. Thick 6px ink rules, no gradients.
STORY: A student sees the name Graphonomicon, understands it is interactive IB Economics graphs, searches or picks Microeconomics. Believes it is unofficial but made for their class.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with three-bar mark + GRAPHONOMICON wordmark, 6px ink rule under it. Headline "Drag the control. Watch the market move." in condensed caps, lede, search below; unit bento beneath. Unofficial-not-affiliated note in a footer on every page.
FORM: Isotype Wall Chart, assigned candidate 3 of the grounded list; seed:14fe78bd5eaa042a.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
